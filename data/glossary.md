# Fictional corpus glossary

- **Northstar:** fictional region used in the dataset.
- **Credits:** fictional currency; figures are not comparable to real currencies.
- **Installed capacity:** rated solar capacity added during a period, measured in megawatts.
- **On-time delivery rate:** share of deliveries arriving on time.
- **Remote consultation:** healthcare consultation conducted without an in-person visit.
- **Source ID:** stable paragraph identifier such as P01. Cite only supplied IDs and distinguish survey results, counts and costs.
